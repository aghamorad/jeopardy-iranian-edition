PAHLAVI MUSIC PACK V3
Original score for the Pahlavi-era Iran quiz-game module.

Core idea
---------
All cues derive from an original five-note motif (D–F–E–A–Eb), transformed across the historical arc:
1921 coup / state-building / Allied occupation / Mossadegh and oil / White Revolution /
the Iranian Left / gender and modernity / Cold War Iran.

Design rules
------------
- Dark, modern, game-show functional.
- No borrowed Jeopardy melody.
- No generic 'Persian' exoticism or santur shorthand.
- No fake archival-radio static.
- No synthwave nostalgia.
- No uncomplicated royal triumphalism.
- Period atmosphere comes from rhythm, orchestration, modernity, pressure and rupture.

Suggested use
-------------
01 Main Theme: title/front-door for Pahlavi module.
02 Thinking Loop: seamless-ish 30s answer clock bed.
03 Daily Double: high-stakes reveal.
04 Final Round: final-question underscore.
05–12: historical/category beds.
13–16: UI stingers.
17 Splash: subdued module entrance.

Technical
---------
44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM, stereo WAV.
