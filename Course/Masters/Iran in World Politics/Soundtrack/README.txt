JEOPARDY! IRAN IN WORLD POLITICS — SOUNDTRACK V2

This version is rebuilt from the course syllabus rather than generic thriller ambience.

Musical architecture:
1. Revolution / popular mobilisation:
   original modal anthem melody, collective strings/brass, restrained march.
2. Iran–Iraq War / state consolidation:
   heavier bass, snare, timpani, low-brass restatement.
3. Institutionalised revolutionary power / foreign policy / Axis of Resistance:
   fragmented theme, more calculated harmony and piano, persistent march pulse.
4. Sanctions / social pressure / present war:
   denser minor-modal harmony, full theme statement, unresolved ending.

Harmonic language:
D minor with E-flat / C-sharp tension; original material, no quotation of existing revolutionary songs.

No radio static, no chanting, no faux-Middle-Eastern ornament, no EDM/trailer drop.
