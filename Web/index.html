How to play

Pick a tile from the board to open a clue. When the clue appears, everyone races to hit their own Buzz button — first one in gets the floor and chooses an answer. Right answers add the clue's value; wrong ones subtract it and hand the clue to everyone else.

On a keyboard, 1 2 3 buzz for contestants one through three, and A–D pick an answer. One tile per round is a Daily Double, and the match closes with Final Jeopardy.
